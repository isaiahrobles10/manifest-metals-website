FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call us — we're happy to talk it through.

How long does a metal roof last in El Paso?

It depends on the system and how it's installed. A 24-gauge standing seam roof typically lasts 40–70 years. On exposed-fastener roofs like R-panel, the panel lasts decades but the screw washers are a maintenance item — in our sun they can start failing at 10–15 years.

Can you replace my shingle roof with metal?

Yes. On our El Paso conversion we tore the shingles off to the bare deck, covered the roof in self-sealing membrane and installed 26-gauge R-panel. How a conversion works.

How much does a metal roof cost?

Every roof is different — size, pitch, what's on it now and which system you choose. We inspect, photograph and measure, then give you a clear written quote.
