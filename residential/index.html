Why homeowners switch

Made for West Texas weather.

  • Built for UV

    Standing seam has no exposed screws — the washers on exposed-fastener roofs start failing at 10–15 years in the sun.

  • Built to last

    A 24-gauge standing seam roof typically lasts 40–70 years.

  • Non-combustible

    Metal won't burn — a layer of protection against embers and sparks.

How we inspect

A different checklist for every kind of roof.

A flat roof and a tile roof fail for completely different reasons, so one generic sheet doesn't cut it. Whatever is on your house now, we inspect it with a form built for that roof.

Shingle roofs

A 10' × 10' hail test square on every slope, plus vents and soft metals — what an adjuster checks.

Tile roofs

On tile, the underlayment is the real roof. We lift tile in at least two places and record what's under it.

Flat & foam roofs

A core test before anything is priced — layer count and deck type decide what's even allowed.

Metal roofs

Hail & insurance

A Class 4 roof can lower your insurance.

Texas law requires home insurers to offer a premium discount for roofs rated UL 2218 Class 4 for impact resistance. Many metal roof systems carry that rating. Each insurer sets its own discount, and after installation the contractor completes TDI form PC068 for your policy.

Ask us which systems qualify — then ask your insurer what they credit.

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call us — we're happy to talk it through.

Can metal go over my existing roof?

It depends on what's there now, how many layers there are and the condition of the deck. We inspect first and tell you what your roof actually needs.

My roof is almost flat. Can it be metal?

Snap-lock standing seam and R-panel both need at least a 3:12 pitch. Flatter roofs need a different system — we'll look at yours and tell you straight.

Which colors can I choose?

Twelve on our R-panel chart — try them in the color visualizer on our home page. We bring physical chips to your site visit.

Do you handle the permit?

Yes. When the job needs a permit, we pull it and schedule the inspections.

How much does it cost?

It depends on size, pitch, what's on the roof now and which system you choose. We inspect, photograph and measure, then give you a clear written quote.
