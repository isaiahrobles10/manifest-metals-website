Systems

Standing seam and R-panel.

Our recommendation for homes

Standing seam

  • Concealed clips — no exposed screws for the sun to attack
  • Clean, modern lines with tall raised seams
  • 24 ga steel: 40–70 year typical service life
Standing seam vs R-panel
Strong & economical

R-panel

  • Ribbed exposed-fastener panel — strong and economical
  • A great fit for shops, barns, outbuildings and commercial
  • Plan on checking fasteners as the roof ages
