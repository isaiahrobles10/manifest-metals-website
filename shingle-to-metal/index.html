What a conversion includes

Six steps, done right.

  1. Inspect the shingle roof

    Hail test squares on every slope, the vents and soft metals, and a look at the deck and attic ventilation.

  2. Tear off to the deck

    On our El Paso conversion the shingles came off down to bare wood, and every bit was hauled away. Any bad decking is replaced — approved by you, in writing.

  3. Seal the whole roof

    Self-sealing waterproof membrane over the entire deck before any metal goes on.

  4. Metal and trim

    Panels, then color-matched ridge, rake and edge trim, and sidewall and transition flashing.

  5. Every penetration

    New seals on pipes and vents, turbines and roof jacks — the places a roof usually leaks.

  6. Clean up and walk it

    Daily cleanup, a magnet sweep of the yard and driveway, and a final walk of the roof with you.

The color decision

The family weighed a special-order Hawaiian Blue against Light Stone, which was in stock. Same roof, same crew — the in-stock color let work start weeks sooner, and the light finish reflects more of the El Paso sun.

Hail & insurance

A Class 4 roof can lower your insurance.

Texas law requires home insurers to offer a premium discount for roofs rated UL 2218 Class 4 for impact resistance. Many metal roof systems carry that rating. Each insurer sets its own discount, and after installation the contractor completes TDI form PC068 for your policy.

Ask us which systems qualify — then ask your insurer what they credit.

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Call us — we're happy to talk it through.

Will you put metal over my shingles?

It depends on the layers and the deck. On our El Paso conversion we removed the shingles down to the deck so we could see the wood, fix what needed fixing and seal the whole roof before the metal went on.

How long does a conversion take?

It depends on the size of the roof and the color you choose — in-stock colors usually start sooner than special orders. We give you a timeline with the written quote.

Does my insurance cover it?

If your shingles have hail damage, it might. We document the roof the way an adjuster checks it — test squares on every slope — so you have a clear record for your claim.

Do you pull the permit?

Yes. When the job needs a permit, we pull it and schedule the inspections.
